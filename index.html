About

Libya Traffic Intelligence

Libya Traffic Intelligence is a data-driven road accident analytics and risk analysis platform built as a graduation-style software engineering project. It processes a synthetic dataset of 2,000 accident records entirely in the browser — parsing CSV, computing statistics, rendering charts and a stylized map from scratch, and demonstrating a transparent rule-based risk-scoring and prediction layer — all without a backend, a framework, or a build step.

All accident records are synthetic and generated for demonstration purposes. They are not official Libyan government statistics.

Technology Stack

HTML5 CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) SVG CSV / Fetch & File APIs No frameworks No build step

Data Science Approach

Records are parsed with a hand-written quoted-field-aware CSV parser, validated, and converted to typed numeric fields; the hour is derived from the time, while weekend and rush-hour flags are validated against the date and the dashboard's defined hours. Analytics functions compute distributions, cross-tabulations, and hotspot rankings on demand, so every chart reacts to the active filter set in real time. The risk score is a transparent weighted sum over severity share, fatality rate, road condition, traffic, lighting, weather, and rush-hour concentration — deliberately readable rather than a black box.

Future Development

  • GPS and traffic-camera data ingestion
